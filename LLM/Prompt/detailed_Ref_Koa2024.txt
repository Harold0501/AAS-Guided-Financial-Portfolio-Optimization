--------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDED FEATURE OPERATIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Use these operations when they match the original feature meaning:

1. Return:
   daily percentage or log change in div_adj_close.

2. Momentum:
   cumulative return over a rolling window.

3. Volatility:
   rolling standard deviation of daily returns.

4. Drawdown:
   decline from rolling maximum price.

5. Breadth:
   fraction of stocks with positive return, rising volume, high volatility, or another condition.

6. Dispersion:
   cross-sectional standard deviation or interquartile range across stock-level quantities.

7. Co-movement:
   average pairwise rolling correlation across stock returns.

8. Reversal:
   comparison between recent return and prior-window return.

9. Volume activity:
   current or rolling volume relative to its own historical rolling average or median.

10. Intraday range:
    high-low range scaled by close or previous close.

11. Gap:
    difference between current open and previous close, scaled by previous close.

12. Portfolio aggregation:
    mean, standard deviation, fraction, average pairwise correlation, or percentile across companies.
